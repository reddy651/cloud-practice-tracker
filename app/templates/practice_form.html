How it works

  1. Create the practice.
  2. Paste one screenshot.
  3. Type the exact step explanation.
  4. Click Save Step.
  5. Paste the next screenshot and repeat.
  6. When finished, click Save Document.
  7. The Word document is created and shown on the practice page.
  8. Click Convert to PDF to create the PDF.
